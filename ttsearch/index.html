Show designs that fit a Pmod. Inferred by matching each project's declared pin names against the recommended pinouts; a match means the pins line up, not that it has been tested.

Tags were assigned by language models from each project's documentation and checked by a second model; they are a browsing aid, not a claim by the authors.

Silicon test results are the reports people file on tinytapeout.com after trying a design on a real chip.
